## Project Overview

This project analyzes 2,823 order lines (Jan 2012 - Aug 2017) across 307 orders to understand revenue, quantity sold, product line, deal size and regional performance.

Overall: **$8.29M revenue | 99,067 units sold | 307 orders**
(The dashboard screenshot shows the Medium deal size view: $4.96M revenue, 52,519 units, 292 orders.)

## Key Insights

- **Classic Cars** is the top product line with $2.97M revenue (36%), followed by Vintage Cars at $1.64M (20%).
- **Medium deals** generate 60% of revenue, Small deals 31% and Large deals 9%.
- **USA** contributes 36% of total revenue; USA, Spain and France together account for about 59%.
- **EMEA** is the largest territory (50% of revenue), followed by North America (38%).
- Revenue **peaked in 2014 ($2.23M)** and declined about 58% by 2016 ($0.94M). 2017 data is partial (till Aug).
- 92% of revenue comes from Shipped orders; Cancelled, On Hold and Disputed orders make up about 4.6%.

## Dashboard Features

- Date range slicer and Deal Size slicer
- DAX measures: Revenue, Quantity Sold, Total Orders, Revenue Contribution %, YTD / QTD / MTD, Last 3 Months
- Multiple report pages: Time Intelligence, Combo Chart, Sunburst, Pie/Donut, Stacked and Clustered charts
